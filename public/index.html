What's your teaching philosophy?

Dancing should be comfortable, expressive, and fun.

It's not just set steps in a syllabus. (It's so much simpler, and more beautiful, than that!) Dancing is a chance to share space, movement, and energy with another person.

You're getting married, right? So, you understand your partner. You communicate. You compromise. You have fun together. These are exactly the characteristics that make good dancers! You're probably a good dancer already — even if you don't know it yet!

Instead of just teaching you a "routine" to memorize and stress over, I'll show you how to move together, easily and beautifully, so you can dance well at your wedding and enjoy dancing together for many years to come!
